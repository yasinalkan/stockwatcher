Data — TradingView public scanner (no API key). Treasury yields, VIX, DXY, gold, silver and FX stream in real time; US equities, ETFs and index futures are delayed ~15 minutes; economic indicators update on their official release schedule. Breadth gauges (S5TW, S5FI, NDFI) are computed here from current index constituents rather than read from a published index, so they may differ slightly from the official prints. Adding a free Finnhub key under the feed menu replaces the delayed equity prices with a live stream; the key is held in this browser only.
Note — This dashboard is an informational tool, not investment advice. Figures are sourced from a third-party public endpoint without any guarantee of accuracy; verify against an official source before acting on them.
