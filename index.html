Treasury yields, VIX, DXY, gold, silver and FX stream in real time; US equities, ETFs and index futures are delayed ~15 minutes; economic indicators update on their official release schedule. Breadth gauges (S5TW, S5FI, NDFI) are computed here from current index constituents rather than read from a published index, so they may differ slightly from the official prints.
